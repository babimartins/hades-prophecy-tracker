# Slice 4 — Fix the interface at 545 entries

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development.

**Goal:** The data is right and the app looks broken. Ten problems found in a usability pass over the live site. All are presentation and interaction; none is a data defect.

**Context that explains all ten:** the interface was designed for 55 prophecies, tuned once for 223 entries, and now carries 545 across 10 collections. Every finding is that gap.

## Global Constraints

`AGENTS.md` is binding — Lit static `properties`, `.js` on relative imports, no `any`, `*.browser.test.ts` for browser tests, clear the DOM in Lit tests with Lit's own render call, dispatch a requirement control on the fact's `kind` and never on the node shape.

- **Do not change data, schema or engine.** `packages/data`, `packages/schema` and `packages/engine` stay untouched. This slice is `apps/web` and `packages/ui` only.
- **Do not change any event contract or public component API** unless a finding requires it; if one does, say so.
- Accessibility does not regress: keyboard operation, visible focus, state exposed to assistive technology, and completion never signalled by colour alone.
- Contrast stays WCAG AA. The palette in `packages/ui/src/tokens.css.ts` is the only source of colour; read it with `colorVar`.
- Commit per finding. Run the suite before each commit.

## The ten findings

Measured on the deployed site at 1280px and confirmed in the DOM.

### 1. Clicking an entry looks like it does nothing — the worst of the ten
The detail view replaces the list, which sits **241px below the fold** under the 13 header cards. The viewport does not move, so the click appears to fail. Fix so opening an entry puts it in view, and so does returning to the list. Do not break the browser Back button if it already works, and do not add routing.

### 2. The progress bar is decoration on 72% of entries
392 of 545 entries have exactly one sub-item, so their bar can only read 0% or 100%: all 174 boons, 72 Daedalus, 26 Well of Charon, 25 keepsakes, 24 aspects, 24 Mirror, 15 Pact, 11 perks. The bar is the widest, heaviest element in the row and carries one bit. Show a bar only where it says something — an entry with two or more sub-items. Give the single-sub-item entry a lighter treatment that still shows done versus not done, without relying on colour alone.

### 3. The top cards have mismatched heights
"Next steps" is about 500px; "Overall" and "Backup" about 105px. The grid row stretches to the tallest and leaves a large void. Make the top row read as deliberate.

### 4. The next-steps panel is cramped
Labels wrap to three lines, the "6 entries" badge wraps to two, and the numeric row has no checkbox so it does not align with the rows above — a number input sits mid-sentence. Give the panel a layout that holds a long label, a control and a count without wrapping into a mess.

### 5. Collapsed sections do not look clickable
A section summary is small-caps text with a small triangle: no background, no border, no obvious hit area. Fifteen of them stack into what reads as dead text. Make a section header look and feel like a control, with a hit area worth tapping.

### 6. "ALL OF" leaks model jargon into the UI
The detail view prints the raw node kind. `all`, `any` and `count` are internal words. Say what the player must do, or show nothing when the meaning is obvious — a flat list of required steps needs no label.

### 7. The display font hurts the headings it is on
Cinzel in small-caps on every card header and section heading is hard to scan at small sizes and wraps "Fated List of Minor Prophecies" onto two lines inside its card. Keep the game feel; reduce the cost. Consider limiting the face to the page title and section headings, or adjusting size, tracking and casing.

### 8. Overall progress never moves
"Overall 0 / 545" counts completed entries, so ticking a sub-item gives no global feedback and the bar will barely crawl across 545 entries. Give the player feedback that reflects what they actually did. Do not change what `overallProgress` computes in the engine — solve it in the interface.

### 9. Sectioned and unsectioned collections behave differently
Sections collapse above 40 rows, but the 55 prophecies have no section and always render flat and open, as do keepsakes, Pact, perks and Well of Charon. The same list behaves two ways for no reason the player can see. Make it consistent.

### 10. Rows are 81px tall
A 174-entry collection is roughly 14,000px. Once finding 2 removes the bar from most rows, tighten the row so a list is scannable.
